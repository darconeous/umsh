This UEP defines how UMSH packets are carried by LoRa radios: the modulation
settings nodes share, how a packet maps onto a LoRa frame, the frame duration
that channel access and forwarding are timed by, the transmit duty cycle, and
how a node contends for the channel before it transmits.

## Channel Parameters {#channel-parameters}

Nodes hear each other only when they share all four of:

| Parameter | Values |
|---|---|
| Center frequency | Any the radio and local rules allow |
| Bandwidth | 125/16, 125/12, 125/8, 125/6, 125/4, 125/3, 62.5, 125, 250, or 500 kHz |
| Spreading factor | 5 to 12 |
| Sync word | [Sync Word](#sync-word) |

The coding rate, 4/5, 4/6, 4/7, or 4/8, travels in each frame's
[header](#modulation), so a receiver decodes any of them. The nodes of a
network still use one coding rate, so that they agree on
[`T_frame`](#t-frame).

A set of nodes that share these parameters forms a network. Which values a
network uses is a matter of deployment and local rules, not of this UEP.

### Sync Word {#sync-word}

UMSH uses the LoRa private-network sync word: `0x12` in the one-byte form of
SX127x radios, `0x1424` in the two-byte form of SX126x radios. The sync word
is shared with other protocols, so it does not by itself identify a frame as
UMSH.

## Modulation {#modulation}

Every frame uses:

- **Explicit header**: the LoRa header carries the frame's length and coding
  rate.
- **PHY CRC**: on. A receiver discards a frame whose CRC fails.
- **IQ**: normal, not inverted.
- **Low data rate optimization**: on when the symbol duration `2^SF / BW` is
  16 ms or longer, and off otherwise. Both ends of a link must agree, so a
  node MUST derive this setting from the rule rather than from its radio's
  default.
- **Preamble**: a sender SHOULD transmit a preamble of 32 symbols. A receiver
  that samples the channel periodically to save power relies on it, and a
  shorter one may go unheard.

## Framing {#framing}

A LoRa frame carries exactly one UMSH packet ([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md)) as its
payload, with nothing added before or after it.

- The **MTU** is 255 bytes, the largest LoRa payload. A sender MUST NOT build
  a packet longer than that.
- A packet received on a LoRa radio is a
  [measured arrival](uep-0012.md#measured).
- A frame whose payload does not begin with a frame control field of a version
  the receiver implements is not a UMSH packet. A node discards it, or hands
  it to another protocol that shares the channel.

## Frame Duration {#t-frame}

**`T_frame`** is the on-air duration of a 255-byte frame at the node's
channel parameters, from the start of its preamble to the end of its CRC.
[Channel access](#channel-access) and forwarding
([UEP-0012](uep-0012.md)) express their timing in multiples of it.

```text
T_sym    = 2^SF / BW
LDRO     = 1 if T_sym ≥ 16 ms, else 0
PL       = 255

for SF ≥ 7:
  bits   = 8 × PL − 4 × SF + 44
  sync   = 4.25
for SF 5 and 6:
  bits   = 8 × PL − 4 × SF + 36
  sync   = 6.25

N_payload = 8 + ceil(bits / (4 × (SF − 2 × LDRO))) × CR_denom
T_frame   = (N_preamble + sync + N_payload) × T_sym
```

`BW` is the exact bandwidth, so 125/16 kHz for the narrowest. `CR_denom` is
the coding rate's denominator, 5 to 8, and `N_preamble` is the node's
transmit preamble in symbols. An implementation MAY round `T_frame` up,
but not down.

## Demodulation Floor {#snr-floor}

The **demodulation floor** is the lowest signal-to-noise ratio at which LoRa
demodulates at a given spreading factor:

| Spreading factor | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Floor (dB) | −2.5 | −5 | −7.5 | −10 | −12.5 | −15 | −17.5 | −20 |

The same SNR is a comfortable margin at one spreading factor and a failing
link at another, so UEPs that judge reception quality measure from the floor.
Bandwidth changes a receiver's sensitivity in dBm, not the SNR it needs.

## Duty Cycle {#duty-cycle}

Where local rules limit the share of time a transmitter may be on the air, a
node MUST NOT exceed that limit. A node that enforces a limit measures its own
transmissions over the past hour, and does not transmit a frame that would
take it over the limit.

## Channel Access {#channel-access}

A node contends for the channel before every transmission, whether
originated, forwarded, or an acknowledgment, except where this section says
otherwise.

### Channel Sensing {#channel-sensing}

Before transmitting, a node SHOULD sense the channel with Channel Activity
Detection (CAD), the LoRa radio's detector for preamble energy. The channel is
also busy while the node is receiving a frame.

- If the channel is idle, the node transmits.
- If the channel is busy, the node [backs off](#backoff).

### Backoff {#backoff}

When the channel is busy, a node:

1. Waits a random time drawn uniformly from `[T_frame / 40, T_frame / 4]`.
2. Senses the channel again.
3. Repeats, up to 16 sensing attempts in all.
4. Drops the frame if the channel is still busy after the last attempt.

A node that withholds a frame for its [duty-cycle limit](#duty-cycle) treats
the attempt as one that found the channel busy.

### Immediate Acknowledgment {#immediate-ack}

A node that sends a [MAC ack](uep-0005.md#mac-ack) for a packet it has just
received SHOULD transmit it as soon as that packet ends, without sensing the
channel, if its radio is free to transmit. The channel was clear when the
packet ended, and forwarders of the same packet hold back by the
[acknowledgment protection interval](#ack-protection) so that the ack has the
channel first. A node whose radio is not free senses the channel and backs off
as for any other frame.

A packet with [source-route hops still pending](uep-0014.md#pending-route-ack)
is not acknowledged, so it draws no immediate acknowledgment.

### Acknowledgment Protection Interval {#ack-protection}

**`D_ack`** is the time a node holds back a transmission that could otherwise
collide with an immediate acknowledgment of the same packet. It SHOULD cover
the destination's receive-to-transmit turnaround plus the airtime of a MAC
ack. The suggested value is `0.25 × T_frame`. An implementation that computes
the MAC ack's airtime MAY use a tighter bound.

CAD alone cannot protect an immediate acknowledgment: a forwarder's contention
delay may be arbitrarily small, CAD detects a preamble and may miss an ack
already past its own, and a forwarder may not hear the destination at all.
`D_ack` gives the acknowledgment a deterministic head start. When it applies
is defined by the UEPs that forward packets, such as
[UEP-0012](uep-0012.md).

## Security Considerations {#security-considerations}

LoRa provides no security of its own. The sync word is public, and the PHY CRC
detects corruption but not tampering, so every guarantee UMSH makes rests on
[UEP-0007](uep-0007.md).

Every node in radio range receives every frame, whether or not it is
addressed. A receiver can estimate where a transmitter is from which nodes
hear it and how strongly, whatever its packets say.

Any transmitter in range can occupy the channel. Jamming, and a node that
ignores channel access or the duty cycle, deny service to every node that
shares the channel, and nothing at this layer prevents it.

An immediate acknowledgment is sent without sensing the channel. A node that
could make others send acknowledgments at will could use them to collide with
other traffic. An acknowledgment answers only an authenticated packet
addressed to the acknowledging node that asked for one
([UEP-0007](uep-0007.md#acknowledgments)). A recorded packet that is
replayed while its counter is recent can draw a
[duplicate acknowledgment](uep-0007.md#duplicate-acknowledgments) each time,
but each costs the attacker a transmission, and draws one short frame.
