This UEP defines how UMSH operates on spectrum where amateur and unlicensed
operation coexist: the three operating modes, what each requires of the
packets a node originates, receives, and forwards, the authority each
transmission is made under, the eligibility rules and the Station Callsign
edit it adds to the [forwarding procedure](uep-0013.md#forwarding-procedure),
and the Operator Callsign and Station Callsign options.

This UEP states protocol behavior. Which mode a node may use, and what each
authority permits on a given frequency, is decided by local rules and by the
node's operator.

## Terminology {#terminology}

**Operating mode**
: Unlicensed, Licensed-Only, or Hybrid, as
  [Operating Modes](#operating-modes) defines.

**Authority**
: What a transmission is made under: **amateur authority** or **unlicensed
  authority**.

**Encrypted packet**
: A packet whose [SCF](uep-0005.md#scf) has `E` set. A broadcast or MAC ack
  carries no SECINFO, and is never encrypted.

**Operator callsign**
: The amateur radio callsign of the operator who originates a packet,
  carried in the [Operator Callsign option](#operator-callsign).

**Carries an Operator Callsign**
: Said of a packet whose first Operator Callsign option has a value that can
  be [interpreted](#operator-callsign).

**Station callsign**
: The amateur radio callsign of the station that transmits a forward,
  carried in the [Station Callsign option](#station-callsign).

## Operating Modes {#operating-modes}

A node operates in one of three modes:

| Mode | Originated packets | Forwarded packets |
|---|---|---|
| **Unlicensed** | Any, under unlicensed authority | Any, under unlicensed authority, with no Station Callsign |
| **Licensed-Only** | Only unencrypted packets that carry an Operator Callsign, under amateur authority | Only unencrypted packets that carry an Operator Callsign, under amateur authority, with this repeater's Station Callsign |
| **Hybrid** | Any. Amateur authority when unencrypted with an Operator Callsign, unlicensed otherwise | Any. As for Licensed-Only when unencrypted with an Operator Callsign, as for Unlicensed otherwise |

A node has one operating mode. It governs the packets the node
[originates](#originated), the packets it [receives](#received), and the
packets it [forwards](#forwarding).

- A node in Licensed-Only mode MUST have an operator callsign.
- A node in Licensed-Only or Hybrid mode that forwards MUST have a station
  callsign.

### Authority {#authority}

Each transmission is made under amateur authority or under unlicensed
authority, as [Originated Packets](#originated) and [Forwarding](#forwarding)
assign. The frequencies, transmit power, and duty cycle each authority
permits are set by local rules. A node transmits within the limits that
local rules set for the authority its transmission is made under, including
any [duty-cycle limit](uep-0012.md#duty-cycle). Which of a node's
transmissions count toward each authority's limits is also decided by local
rules.

Encryption takes precedence: an encrypted packet is never transmitted under
amateur authority, whatever options it carries.

## Originated Packets {#originated}

A packet a node originates, a MAC ack among them, follows the node's
operating mode:

- **Unlicensed**: no restriction from this UEP. The packet is transmitted
  under unlicensed authority.
- **Licensed-Only**: the node MUST NOT originate an encrypted packet, and
  every packet it originates MUST carry an Operator Callsign. The packet is
  transmitted under amateur authority.
- **Hybrid**: no restriction from this UEP. A packet that is unencrypted and
  carries an Operator Callsign is transmitted under amateur authority, and
  any other under unlicensed authority.

A node MAY include an Operator Callsign in any mode, on an encrypted packet
as well as an unencrypted one.

The Operator Callsign is static, so it is fixed when the packet is sealed: a
MAC ack's [`ack_tag`](uep-0007.md#ack-tag) covers it, as a packet's MIC and
frame signature do. A node in Licensed-Only mode includes its Operator
Callsign in a MAC ack before computing the tag.

An originator MUST NOT include a Station Callsign. On the originator's own
transmission of a packet that carries an Operator Callsign, the station is
the one its Operator Callsign names.

## Received Packets {#received}

A node in Licensed-Only mode MUST discard every encrypted packet it receives,
whatever its packet type. It reads the `E` bit before
[authentication](uep-0007.md#receive-procedure) and does no cryptographic
work on the packet: it does not decrypt, deliver, acknowledge, answer, or
forward it, and records nothing from it, such as a route, a peer's liveness,
or a frame counter. It counts the discard.

Unlicensed and Hybrid modes add no rule for received packets.

## Forwarding {#forwarding}

This UEP adds rules to stage 1 and step 4 of the
[forwarding procedure](uep-0013.md#forwarding-procedure), and applies them to
[routed and flood hops](uep-0013.md#hop-kinds) alike. A repeater applies the
rules of its operating mode to each forward it transmits on a radio link.
The UEP that defines any other link states how that link treats them.

### Eligibility {#eligibility}

These rules join [stage 1](uep-0013.md#eligibility). A repeater in
Licensed-Only mode declines a packet that:

- is encrypted; or
- does not carry an Operator Callsign.

Each is a [decline](uep-0013.md#stage-outcomes). Unlicensed and Hybrid modes
decline nothing at this stage.

A forward is then made under this authority:

| Mode | Packet | Authority | Station Callsign edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlicensed | Any | Unlicensed | Remove |
| Licensed-Only | Unencrypted, carries an Operator Callsign | Amateur | Set |
| Hybrid | Unencrypted, carries an Operator Callsign | Amateur | Set |
| Hybrid | Any other | Unlicensed | Remove |

### The Station Callsign Edit {#station-callsign-edit}

The Station Callsign edit is step 4 of the
[edits to the packet](uep-0013.md#edits), a fixed-size edit made on routed and
flood hops alike.

- **Set**: the forward carries exactly one Station Callsign option, whose
  value is this repeater's station callsign. It replaces every Station
  Callsign option the packet carried, or is inserted when the packet carried
  none.
- **Remove**: the forward carries no Station Callsign option.

The repeater MUST make the edit its forward calls for.

Setting a Station Callsign can lengthen the packet, by inserting the option
or by replacing a shorter value, and removing one shortens it. The change
counts toward the fixed-size measurement of
[link fit](uep-0013.md#link-fit). A repeater does not omit or shorten the
edit to make room, and a packet that does not fit is declined as link fit
defines.

The inserted option record takes at most 9 bytes: one header byte and a
value of up to 8 bytes, since the delta from the Operator Callsign (4), or
any later option below 15, fits the header's nibble
([UEP-0003](uep-0003.md#option-records)). The one byte a traced packet
leaves ([UEP-0016](uep-0016.md#trace-headroom)) is not enough for a
repeater that inserts or lengthens a Station Callsign.

### Identity and Confirmation {#identity}

The Operator Callsign is static, and is part of a packet's
[forwarding identity](uep-0013.md#forwarding-identity). The Station Callsign
is dynamic, and is not: copies that differ only in their Station Callsign
share a forwarding identity, and a copy whose Station Callsign the next
repeater set or removed is a [confirming copy](uep-0013.md#confirming-copy).

### Repeaters without This UEP {#interoperation}

The Station Callsign option is forwarding-critical, so a repeater that does
not implement this UEP does not forward a packet that carries one
([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#criticality)). Once a repeater has set a Station
Callsign, the packet crosses only repeaters that implement this UEP, until
one forwards it under unlicensed authority and removes the option. A packet
as originated carries no Station Callsign, so a repeater that does not
implement this UEP can forward it, and that forward carries no Station
Callsign. The Operator
Callsign is neither forwarding- nor delivery-critical, and every repeater
carries it unchanged.

## Operator Callsign Option (4) {#operator-callsign}

The amateur radio callsign of the operator who originated the packet.

- **Attributes**: static ([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#option-attributes)). It is
  covered by the packet's MIC and frame signature, and a MAC ack's
  [`ack_tag`](uep-0007.md#ack-tag).
- **Value**: the callsign in [ARNCE](uep-0003.md#arnce), 2, 4, 6, or 8 bytes.
  A sender encodes it in the fewest bytes that hold it.
- **Repeats**: a sender MUST NOT include more than one. A receiver reads the
  first and ignores the rest. A forwarder carries them all unchanged, as it
  does every static option.
- **Malformed value**: a value that is not a valid ARNCE encoding of a
  non-empty callsign, including one of any other length, cannot be
  interpreted ([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#criticality)). A packet whose first
  Operator Callsign is malformed does not
  [carry an Operator Callsign](#terminology).

## Station Callsign Option (15) {#station-callsign}

The amateur radio callsign of the station that transmitted the forward.

- **Attributes**: forwarding-critical and dynamic
  ([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#option-attributes)). It is not covered by the MIC,
  and each repeater sets or removes it as
  [The Station Callsign Edit](#station-callsign-edit) defines.
- **Value**: the callsign in [ARNCE](uep-0003.md#arnce), 2, 4, 6, or 8 bytes.
  A repeater encodes it in the fewest bytes that hold it.
- **Repeats**: a receiver reads the first and ignores the rest. A forward
  carries at most one, as the edit defines.
- **Malformed value**: a value that is not a valid ARNCE encoding of a
  non-empty callsign, including one of any other length, in any occurrence,
  cannot be interpreted ([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#criticality)). A repeater
  does not forward the packet, and its delivery is unaffected.

## Security Considerations {#security-considerations}

**Callsigns are claims.** Nothing binds a callsign to a license holder or to
a node's key. An Operator Callsign is authenticated only as far as the
packet's static options are: by the MIC to whoever holds the packet's key, by
a frame signature to the signer, and on a MAC ack by `ack_tag` to the two
endpoints ([UEP-0007](uep-0007.md#ack-tag)). An unsigned broadcast
authenticates nothing, and on an
[unsigned multicast](uep-0007.md#unsigned-multicast) any member of the group
can attach any Operator Callsign to any `SRC`. An Operator Callsign does not
ensure that every hop was made by a repeater implementing this UEP
([Repeaters without This UEP](#interoperation)).

**Authority follows claims.** A repeater cannot verify an Operator Callsign,
so any node can cause a repeater in Licensed-Only or Hybrid mode to forward
an unencrypted packet under amateur authority, with that repeater's Station
Callsign, by adding one, including to a copy of another node's packet that
its destination will reject. In Hybrid mode, adding or
removing one changes the authority, and so the limits, a repeater forwards
under. A repeater MAY decline such packets under its local policy
([UEP-0013](uep-0013.md#eligibility)).

**Station Callsign.** The Station Callsign is dynamic and unauthenticated.
Any node in range can add, change, or remove one, misidentifying the station
that transmitted a forward. Adding one to a copy stops that copy at every
repeater that does not implement this UEP, and adding a malformed one stops
it at every repeater. Other copies of a flood are unaffected.

**Unencrypted operation.** An unencrypted packet exposes its payload to every
listener, and an unencrypted multicast or group unicast exposes its source,
and a group unicast its destination, to nodes outside the group
([UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#security-considerations)). On a packet that carries
a MIC, its recipients still detect forgery and alteration by anyone without
its key ([UEP-0007](uep-0007.md#mic)). Repeaters, which hold no key, do not.
A node in Licensed-Only mode [discards](#received) every encrypted packet
unopened and forwards none, so none of its transmissions discloses what an
encrypted packet concealed. An unencrypted packet is one whose SCF has `E`
clear. This UEP does not examine its payload.

**Observability.** Options are never encrypted. Whether a packet is
encrypted, and the callsigns it carries, can be read from every packet by
any listener, so any listener can tell which authority this UEP assigns to
each transmission.

**Linkability.** An Operator Callsign names a person, publicly. A node that
includes one, on encrypted traffic as well as unencrypted, links all of that
traffic to its operator, whatever its node hint or encryption conceal.

## Appendix A: Test Vectors {#test-vectors}

Values are hexadecimal.

### Callsigns {#callsign-vectors}

Option values in their shortest [ARNCE](uep-0003.md#arnce) form:

| Callsign | Value |
|---|---|
| `N0CALL` | `5B BB 08 2C` |
| `KZ2X` | `48 ED 96 00` |
| `KZ2Y` | `48 ED 9C 40` |
| `AB1CDEFGHIJK` | `06 AC 13 65 26 A0 39 DB` |

The MAC ack in [UEP-0005](uep-0005.md#mac-ack-vector) and
[UEP-0007](uep-0007.md#ack-vector) that carries option 4 carries the
Operator Callsign `N0CALL`, and UEP-0007 gives its `AAD_ack` and `ack_tag`.

### Station Callsign Edit {#edit-vectors}

A repeater in Licensed-Only mode, with repeater hint `30 30` and
station callsign `N0CALL`, forwards an unencrypted request and its MAC ack.
It records itself in each packet's trace, having received each copy at an
RSSI of −1 dBm or stronger and an SNR of 0 dB. The MIC and the ack trailer
are copied unchanged, so these vectors do not depend on the keys that
produced them.

**Flood hop.** A `UNAR` from node hint `10 10 10` to node hint `20 20 20`,
with `FHOPS` `20`, unencrypted with a 16-byte MIC, frame counter
`0A 09 08 07`, carrying Operator Callsign `KZ2X`, an empty Trace, and the
payload `hello`:

| Field | Received | Forwarded |
|---|---|---|
| FCF | `D9` | `D9` |
| FHOPS | `20` | `11` |
| DST, SRC | `20 20 20`, `10 10 10` | Unchanged |
| SECINFO | `60 0A 09 08 07` | Unchanged |
| Operator Callsign (4) | `44 48 ED 96 00` | Unchanged |
| Trace (7) | `30` | `34 30 30 01 00` |
| Station Callsign (15) | | `84 5B BB 08 2C` |
| End marker, payload | `FF 68 65 6C 6C 6F` | Unchanged |
| MIC | 16 bytes | Unchanged |

```text
Received:  D9 20 20 20 20 10 10 10 60 0A 09 08 07 44 48 ED
           96 00 30 FF 68 65 6C 6C 6F 57 8D 58 FF 1B 55 23
           78 05 99 85 01 71 65 8E E5
Forwarded: D9 11 20 20 20 10 10 10 60 0A 09 08 07 44 48 ED
           96 00 34 30 30 01 00 84 5B BB 08 2C FF 68 65 6C
           6C 6F 57 8D 58 FF 1B 55 23 78 05 99 85 01 71 65
           8E E5
```

**Routed hop.** The destination's MAC ack, source-routed through the
repeater, carrying Operator Callsign `KZ2Y` and an empty Trace. The repeater
removes its hint from the Source Route, inserts its Station Callsign, and
records itself in the trace:

| Field | Received | Forwarded |
|---|---|---|
| FCF | `C8` | `C8` |
| Source Route (3) | `32 30 30` | `30` |
| Operator Callsign (4) | `14 48 ED 9C 40` | Unchanged |
| Trace (7) | `30` | `34 30 30 01 00` |
| Station Callsign (15) | | `84 5B BB 08 2C` |
| ACK MIC, ACK TAG | `57 8D 58 FF A0 93 F7 44` | Unchanged |

```text
Received:  C8 32 30 30 14 48 ED 9C 40 30 57 8D 58 FF A0 93
           F7 44
Forwarded: C8 30 14 48 ED 9C 40 34 30 30 01 00 84 5B BB 08
           2C 57 8D 58 FF A0 93 F7 44
```
