PROP_MESHCORE_REGION_POLICY values: what happens to an unscoped flood, and to a scoped one whose first transport code does or does not match a region in PROP_MESHCORE_REGIONS.

Constants§

IGNORE
Every flood is forwarded, whatever its scope.
OPEN
Unscoped and matching floods are forwarded; unmatched are not.
SCOPED_ONLY
Only matching floods are forwarded.
