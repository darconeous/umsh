CAP_WIFI_AP: the device can bring up an access point of its own (PROP_WIFI_AP_ENABLED, PROP_WIFI_AP_CONFIG, PROP_WIFI_AP_STATE, PROP_WIFI_AP_CLIENTS).

Requires WIFI_SCAN and not WIFI. Nearly every chip does both, but a station and an access point are different functions with different state, and a device that has one and not the other is describable.
