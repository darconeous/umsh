Location the device identity advertises (PROP_IDENT_LOCATION) — 0–7 octets in the variable-precision interleaved format, empty to advertise none. The value carries its own precision, so a location entered by hand needs no separate precision setting.

This and IDENT_ALTITUDE are where the advertised identity gets its position, whether a fix wrote them or an administrator did. GNSS_IDENT_UPDATE set, the device writes them itself from each fix and refuses a write with STATUS_INVALID_STATE; cleared, they are writable and hold whatever they last held. Requires CAP_IDENT, not CAP_GNSS — a fixed repeater with no receiver still has somewhere to be.
