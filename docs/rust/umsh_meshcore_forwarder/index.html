A MeshCore v1 forwarder.

An interoperable repeater for MeshCore v1 traffic, sharing a radio with a UMSH node. It parses MeshCore frames, suppresses duplicates, and forwards exactly what a baseline MeshCore repeater would forward, mutated exactly as the baseline mutates it, so that no neighbor can tell from what it receives that the repeater is not a MeshCore one.

The wire behavior follows MeshCore’s repeater-v1.17.1 release, commit BASELINE_COMMIT. Local policy—airtime estimation, channel access, duty limiting, randomness, and table and queue sizes—belongs to the device it runs on and reaches the engine through Environment and the caller’s own transmit path.

The engine holds the device’s public key and nothing else of its identity. Its only cryptography is SHA-256, HMAC-SHA256 for region codes, and Ed25519 verification of advertisements: nothing here signs, agrees on a key, or decrypts, and nothing originates a packet.
