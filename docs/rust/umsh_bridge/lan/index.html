Module lan

A participant’s membership in a LAN bearer group, and the loop that relays frames between it and one queue in each direction.

Every datagram is a frame, with no header and no measurements: what arrives from the group crosses as an unmeasured reception, and what leaves for it is the frame alone.

No repeater stands between the group and the bridge, so nothing else stops a frame circulating between two bridge participants on the same LAN, or between this one and its own multicast echo. The relay therefore carries a frame across at most once, in either direction, within CROSSING_WINDOW. That drops nothing a repeater would have carried: every frame it suppresses is one the far side’s duplicate caches have already seen.
