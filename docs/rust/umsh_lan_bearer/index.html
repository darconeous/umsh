UMSH frames over IP multicast on a local network.

The LAN bearer carries one frame per UDP datagram, with no header, to an IPv4 multicast group every participant joins. Radios on the LAN forward between the group and their LoRa meshes; desktops and phones reach the mesh through them. Participants advertise themselves, and the LoRa settings they use, over DNS-SD.

  • classify and the constants: the datagram rules every participant applies.
  • txt: the DNS-SD TXT schema, no_std so a radio’s responder and a host’s browser share one codec.
  • socket and radio (feature tokio): the group membership with echo suppression, and a [umsh_hal::Radio] over it.
  • dnssd (feature dnssd): advertising and browsing.
