Async, allocation-free drivers for the Pager’s peripheral chips. Register references and the hardware qualification checklist are in docs/hardware/lilygo-t-lora-pager-hardware.md.

Modules§

alert
Pager locate outputs. ES8311 register sequencing follows Espressif’s Apache-2.0 esp-bsp/components/es8311 driver; fixed 4.096 MHz MCLK / 16 kHz.
display
ST7796 controller, 480×222 landscape window. Transport handles bus sharing.
gauge
BQ27220 access control and read-only RAM inspection (TI SLUUBD4A, chapters 5–6). Battery parameters are provisioned externally; startup never rewrites them.
input
Quadrature policy and TCA8418 key events. Quadrature transition is called from the GPIO interrupt handler on BOTH edges of BOTH inputs.
input_power
Input/display handoff policy, independent of GPIO and the executor.
power
XL9555 power domains and BQ25896/BQ27220 telemetry.
rtc
PCF85063A, 24-hour mode, 2000–2099. Oscillator-stop means unknown time.
