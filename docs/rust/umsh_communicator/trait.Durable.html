Expand description

What a MemoryStore keeps its records in beyond its own memory.

The store hands each record it changes to the layer, encoded and under its own key, at the end of every step of a commit and of every other change. What the layer keeps therefore follows the commit order, and power lost partway through leaves it where the commit order says is safe. Mounting reads every record back.
