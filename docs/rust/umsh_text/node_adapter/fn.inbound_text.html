Expand description

Classify a received frame for a chat session whose identity is local_key.

None for anything that is not a text frame a chat session holds: another payload type, a unicast or blind unicast without its sender’s key, a multicast without a claimed hint, or another packet family.

The checks on the well-known channels live here, at the chat layer, rather than under the MAC: the requirements are about chat messages, and the MAC is deliberately incurious about what it carries. A frame that fails one is refused rather than shown unmarked—a message the reader would act on in an emergency must not arrive with its origin or its reach in question. EMERGENCY chat must also be signed, but the text-message format has no signature yet, so that requirement is neither sent nor checked.
