pub fn reject( &mut self, batch: PendingBatch, checkpoints: &[StreamCheckpoint], seeds: Seeds, now_ms: u64, ) -> Result<Drain, BatchMismatch>

Give up on a batch that could not be stored.

Its frames are failed, and the engine is rebuilt from checkpoints— what storage holds—so nothing it composed since is remembered as sent. The channels, the resolved members, and the Sender Handle are the session’s, not the engine’s, and are kept.

The returned drain holds everything produced up to the rebuild, named by the old session. The rebuilt engine runs as seeds.session_id, which must differ from the old one: the new engine reissues handles and transmission IDs from the start.
