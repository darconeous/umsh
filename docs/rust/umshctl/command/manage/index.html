manage: administering another device over the mesh.

Every other command in this tool speaks ULCP down the wire to the radio it is attached to. This one borrows that radio and becomes a node: it runs a host MAC over the attachment, and speaks the same ULCP grammar to a device across the room, carried by the Node Management binding (docs/protocol/src/app-node-management.md).

Two identities are in play and it is worth keeping them apart. The attached radio has its own device identity, which identity shows. This tool has a separate, persistent administrator identity — admin-key prints it — and a device is managed from here only once that key is listed in its PROP_DEV_ADMINS, which dev-admin add does over a bench link.
