A ULCP property client: a cached model of a device’s settings, kept over an exchange that carries requests to the device and its answers back.

  • exchange: what a link to a device does—take a request, hand back its answer, and pass on whatever the device publishes.
  • local: the device’s own session, reached in process. A local binding answers every request exactly once, so the link has no timeouts.
  • descriptor: the properties the model covers, each with its codec from [umsh_ulcp], its capability gate, and its kind.
  • model: the cache, the requests waiting to go out, and the writes waiting on their answers.

Nothing here assumes that an answer arrives at once, or at all: the same model serves a device across the mesh, where a request may take a minute and its answer may never come.
