Module lan 

Source
Expand description

The LAN virtual radio: a soft device whose PHY is the LAN bearer’s multicast group.

What the device transmits is sent to the group, where every radio with its bearer enabled hears it; what any participant sends to the group is received unmeasured, since nothing here measured it. The device lives for as long as its owner runs it, whether or not a host is attached: detached, it goes on receiving, acknowledging for its host, and queueing, exactly as a companion radio does.

The group is the device’s own PROP_LAN_BEARER_GROUP, joined while its PHY is on, and PROP_LAN_BEARER_STATE says how that went: a host moves the radio to another group by writing the property, as it would on a radio that forwards.

The host’s link is the caller’s. run takes the host’s side as HostEvents, each attach bringing the sink its replies go to, so one driver serves an in-memory pipe, a local socket (serve), and anything else that carries HDLC-Lite.
