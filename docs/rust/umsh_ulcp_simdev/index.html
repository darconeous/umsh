Soft ULCP devices: the production device [Session] behind an HDLC-Lite byte stream, with no hardware under it.

soft::SoftDevice is the core, generic over a soft::SoftPlatform that supplies storage, entropy, a clock, and peripherals. Two platforms ship here:

  • SimulatedDevice: a deterministic test fixture. Storage is RAM, identities come from a counter, and the battery, receiver, Wi-Fi station, and I2C bus are canned. Every protocol decision is still made by the real session. Transmissions accumulate until SimulatedDevice::take_transmitted drains them, so the caller decides what “the air” is—the web debugger discards it, a bridge copies it to real segments.
  • host::HostPlatform: a real device with no radio of its own, such as a LAN virtual radio. State persists in files, identities come from the operating system’s generator, and the clock is the system’s.

The SessionConfig is the caller’s: it names the device and declares its capability surface. A simulated device has no node behind it, so its configuration must leave SessionConfig::mac_node unset—a backhaul would connect the host to nothing.
